# Community Conduct

Participate with respect, technical care, and patience. State assumptions and
evidence, welcome questions from people at different experience levels, and
focus criticism on artifacts and reasoning rather than individuals.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
exposure of private information, repeated disruption, or pressure to disclose
confidential circuits and model data is not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove violating material and may restrict participation
when necessary. Responses should be proportionate and protect reporters from
retaliation. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's
published GitHub contact channel; disclose conflicts so a different maintainer
can handle the report.
